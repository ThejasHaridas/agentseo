# AgentRank: cinematic brag plan

## The rubric

- **What it is:** AgentRank audits a website for classic SEO and for AI agents, and writes the fixes.
- **Who it's for, what it does for them:** site owners and agencies whose next customers arrive through ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and browser agents. It shows what those agents can read, what they can do, whether they recommend you, and how to fix it.
- **What sets it apart:** it doesn't just score you. A real browser agent tries your key tasks and records every step, AI models are asked your customers' questions, and the fixes come ready to paste.
- **Most impressive claim:** a live AI agent tries your booking flow, shows where it got stuck, and never submits anything.
- **Visual hook:** a black screen and one sentence: "Your next customer isn't a person."
- **Real UI to show:** the app's own components, rendered with `frontend/app/globals.css`: the finding row ("Prices · Not found"), the agent-replay filmstrip with a stuck frame, "Named in all answers", the Agent-Ready gauge with the after-fixes arc, and "Fix first".
- **Tone:** cinematic: trailer-scale type, dramatic colour wipes, a braam on the reveal.
- **Share caption:** "Your next customer isn't a person. AgentRank shows you what AI agents see on your site, then writes the fixes."

## Angle

A trailer for a new kind of customer. The agent is the protagonist; AgentRank is how you see through its eyes. Four verbs carry the middle: it reads, it tests, it asks, it fixes.

## Identity

The product's own: warm monochrome (`#fbfbfa` canvas, `#111` ink), Newsreader for statements and figures, Geist for text, the app's pastel fields (red, green, blue and rose) as full-screen chapter colours, ink-blue `#1f6c9f` for data. The real logo mark (bars and cursor).

## Music

An original score, synthesised: 96 BPM in A minor, so one bar is exactly 2.5 s and the 20 s film is exactly 8 bars. Every cut lands on a bar line; every effect sits on the same grid, counted from frame 0.

- Bars 1–2: low drone, ticking eighths, a sub hit on each downbeat, a riser through bar 2.
- Bar 3: impact and braam on the reveal.
- Bars 4–6: four-on-the-floor toms, a staccato string ostinato, and a whoosh into each wipe.
- Bar 7: the ostinato lifts and a riser builds.
- Bar 8: final impact and braam, decaying to silence.
