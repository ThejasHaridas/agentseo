# AgentRank: vertical cinematic cut (9:16, 50 s)

The 20-second landscape trailer, re-cut for phones and extended to 50 s at the user's request (beyond
/brag-slim's usual 15–25 s). Same angle, identity and score key; 1080×1920, 30 fps.

**Grid:** 96 BPM in A minor, one bar = 2.5 s, 50 s = exactly 20 bars. Every cut lands on a bar line.

Readability: every headline settles within 0.5 s of its bar line and holds at least 1.6 s; longer lines get two bars.

Transitions: a new field wipes up from the bottom and lands on the bar line (quart in-out, 0.5 s). Within one field, the old content leaves first (0.3 s), then the new content rises. No cross-fades.

Fictional stand-ins: Halden Coffee, its numbers and the competitor domains; log IPs come from documentation ranges.
